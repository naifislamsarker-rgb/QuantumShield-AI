# QuantumShield AI

**AI-Powered Quantum Risk Intelligence Platform**

QuantumShield AI is an AI-powered quantum risk intelligence platform designed to help Bangladesh's digital ecosystem prepare for the emerging quantum computing era.

## The Problem
Advances in quantum computing may threaten widely used public-key cryptographic systems, creating a need for organizations to understand their cryptographic exposure and prepare for post-quantum security.

## Our Solution
QuantumShield AI aims to help organizations:
- Assess their quantum-readiness.
- Identify potential cryptographic risks.
- Understand post-quantum cryptography migration considerations.
- Receive actionable security insights through an intelligent security copilot.

## Initial Target Market
Microfinance institutions in Bangladesh, with potential expansion into healthcare, government, education, and industry.

## Key Features
- Quantum-readiness dashboard
- Cryptographic risk assessment
- Post-quantum migration guidance
- AI security copilot
- Risk reporting and recommendations
